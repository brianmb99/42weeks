# 42 Weeks — India Working Notes

> Supporting notes for the 2027–28 family sabbatical. Keep detailed India planning here rather than in the main plan.

## Current itinerary concept

1. Arrive through Delhi.

Keep the route flexible enough to attend a cricket match. India's November 2027 international fixtures and venues are not yet published; a strong domestic match is the fallback if the international calendar or geography does not fit.

## Trek ideas

### Nag Tibba — leading practical candidate

- Near Dehradun
- Two days
- Approximately 3,000 m
- Easy-to-moderate and described as suitable for families/groups
- Limited disruption to family time and work
- Offers a real Himalayan experience without consuming a full week

### Deoriatal–Chandrashila — more ambitious alternative

- Roughly six days in the official itinerary
- Approximately 3,700 m at the high point
- More dramatic mountain panorama
- Longer drives, longer hiking days, and a larger commitment
- Better if the trek is intended to be a defining experience of the India leg

## Planning logic

- Put Dehradun and the trek first because higher routes become less predictable as winter advances.
- Northern and western India are generally seasonally attractive from late October onward as the southwest monsoon withdraws.
- Keep the itinerary deliberately selective so Brian can complete eight-hour workdays from stable accommodations.
- Avoid an extended Delhi stay in November or December if air quality is poor; monitor conditions closer to travel.
- Southern India remains an optional finish, but Tamil Nadu's northeast monsoon makes the southeast coast less predictably dry in this window. Kerala is generally more attractive by November and December.
