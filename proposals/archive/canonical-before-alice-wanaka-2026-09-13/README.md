## Why it was superseded

The archived route remained a legitimate compact plan, but it:

- reached Australia too late for a robust MCG match window,
- gave Newtown only six nights,
- treated the outback as a six-night late-October stop,
- gave Sydney only one week,
- used fourteen nights for Singapore and twenty for Hong Kong,
- and omitted New Zealand.

The selected replacement uses an August 21 weekend departure, a seven-night Newtown stay, the road loop over Labor Day, fourteen nights each in Alice Springs and Sydney, shorter office stays, and a twenty-night Wānaka working base.
